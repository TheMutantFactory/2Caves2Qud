# Rift Wizard 3 update, 2026-09-04

Steam build 23995367 (2026-07-01) to 24995190 (2026-09-04). Backup of the
old install at `C:\Users\danie\rw3-backups\RiftWizard3_2026-09-04_0309`;

## What changed in the game

- 17 of 23 source files changed; RiftWizard3.py alone +1279/-734 lines.
- Music switched from `.ogg` to `.mp3` (18 files). Same names.
- 105 new character sprites: wardrobe outfits for the player (`player_*`),
  new rare and final bosses (Hydra, Orochi, Twin Dragon, The Body Electric,
  Amaru, Maweth, Great Faery, Gryla, Rainbow Slime, Prismancer...), and
  5 removed (apocalypse_beetle, sasquatch, othermind, gate, big_test).
- 101 new UI images, mostly achievements and wardrobe unlock art.
- Equipment overhauled (+967/-476 lines), Components expanded, Mutators
  gained weather and wardrobe outfits, LevelRewards gained new shrines.
- LevelGen only touched `add_more_monsters`; the generator still runs headless.
- ModHandler gained `register_component` and `register_tag` for mods.

## Picked up

- **Wardrobe** (Godot menu, `W`): choose any of the 78 `player_*` outfits;
  saved to `user://drift.cfg` and worn by the kart, the survivors hero and
  the gauntlet wizard. `--mode=wardrobe` opens it from the command line.
- **Boss racers**: campaign realms 10 and 15 seed a rare monster into the
  grid, realm 20 one of the 12 final bosses (5x5 sprites, stat line 6/9,
  HP `boss_hp_base + boss_hp_per_realm * realm`). `--realm=N` on the race
  scene jumps straight there.
- **Survivors** accepts the radius-2 final bosses at realm 20 (HP capped by
  `survivors.boss_hp_cap`).
